Privacy Policy

privacy policy This policy explains what information we collect when you use Kodie apps, sites, services, products, and content (“Services”). It also has information about how we store, use, transfer, and delete that information. Our aim is not just to comply with privacy law. It’s to earn your trust.

Information We Collect & How We Use It Kodie doesn’t make money from ads. So, we don’t collect data in order to advertise to you. The tracking we do at Kodie is to make our service work as well as possible.

In order to give you the best possible experience using KODIE, we collect information from you and your customers using our service. Some of this information, you actively provide us when orders are placed (such as your billing email address or your customer’s email address, product information. Other information, we collect based on actions you take while using KODIE, such as app installation, invoicing and order creation. This information includes records of those actions, your Internet Protocol address, information about your device (such as device or browser type), and referral information.

We use this information to:

  • Provide, test, improve, promote and personalize KODIE Services
  • Fight security issues and other forms of abuse
  • Generate aggregate, non-identifying information about how consumers use KODIE Services When you create your KODIE account, we may collect, store, and periodically update information associated with your account.

Information Disclosure KODIE won’t transfer information about you to third parties for the purpose of providing or facilitating third-party advertising to you. We won’t sell information about you. We may share your account information with third parties in some circumstances, including:

  • With your consent;
  • To a service provider or partner who meets our data protection standards;
  • With academic or non-profit researchers, with aggregation, anonymization, or pseudonymization;
  • When we have a good faith belief it is required by law, such as pursuant to a subpoena or other legal process;
  • When we have a good faith belief that doing so will help prevent imminent harm to someone. If we are going to share your information in response to the legal process, we’ll give you notice so you can challenge it (for example by seeking court intervention), unless we’re prohibited by law or believe doing so may endanger others. We will object to requests for information about users of our services that we believe are improper.

Data Storage KODIE uses third-party vendors and hosting partners, such as AWS, for hardware, software, networking, storage, and related technology we need to run KODIE. We maintain two types of logs: application logs and server logs. By using KODIE Services, you authorize KODIE to transfer, store, and use your information in the United States and any other country where we operate.

Tracking & Cookies We use browser cookies and similar technologies to recognize you when you return to our Services. We use them in various ways, for example, to log you in, remember your preferences, and personalized service and other information. KODIE doesn’t track you across the Internet. We track only your interactions within the KODIE service.

Modifying or Deleting Your Personal Information If you have a KODIE account, you can uninstall your app. or delete your account by contacting us at support@encomm.com. To protect information from accidental or malicious destruction, we may maintain residual copies for a brief time period. But, if you delete your account, your information and content will be unrecoverable after that time. KODIE may preserve and maintain copies of your information when required to do so by law.

Data Security We use encryption (HTTPS/TLS) to protect data transmitted to and from our site. However, no data transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, so we can’t guarantee security. You use the Service at your own risk, and you’re responsible for taking reasonable measures to secure your account.

Business Transfers If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, bankruptcy, reorganization or sale of assets such that your information would be transferred or become subject to a different privacy policy, we’ll notify you in advance so you can opt out of any such new policy by deleting your account before transfer.

Email from KODIE Sometimes we’ll send you emails about your account, service changes, or new policies. You can’t opt-out of this type of “transactional” email (unless you delete your account). But, you can opt-out of non-administrative emails such as digests, newsletters, and activity notifications through your account’s Settings tab. When you interact with an email sent from KODIE (such as opening an email or clicking on a particular link in an email), we may receive information about that interaction. We won’t email you to ask for your password or other account information. If you receive such an email, please send it to us so we can investigate.

Changes to this Policy KODIE may periodically update this Policy. We’ll notify you about significant changes to it.

Data Protection Statement for European Union Users Description of Processing Activity KODIE collects and stores personal information about its customers and their end consumers to efficiently handle subscription orders and optimize conversion rates on your store. It shares minimal data with its service providers. KODIE may periodically update this Policy. We’ll notify you about significant changes to it.

Purposes of Processing

  • Provide, test, promote, and improve the services
  • Gather usage statistics of services
  • Fight spam, fraud, and other abuse of services

Legal Bases In order to provide the services, KODIE collects and stores personal data about its users on the legal basis of consent given when you create an account and agree to the Privacy Policy.

Categories of Recipients KODIE shares minimal personal data with third-party processors in order to provide the Services. These processors offer at least the same level of data protection as that set out in this statement. This includes the following categories of recipients:

  • Hosting, Storage, & Other Infrastructure
  • Analytics
  • Communication & Support
  • Payment Processors

Retention KODIE retains personal data associated with your account for the lifetime of your account. If you would like to delete your personal information, you can delete your account at any time. To delete your payment or billing information, you will need to do so with your payment provider, as KODIE only has minimal secure access to those records as needed to provide the services.
