Online Gift-Giving Trends: Understanding Consumer Behavior and Maximizing Marketing Opportunities

By Bhavana Krishnamurthy · July 3, 2023

Online Gift Giving: An Overview The advent of e-commerce has made online gift-giving an increasingly popular option. It allows consumers to shop from the comfort of their own homes and eliminates the need to visit physical stores. Online gift-giving has become particularly popular across the globe during the COVID-19 pandemic, as more people have been staying at home and avoiding in-person gatherings.

Recent studies show that Online gift-giving is expected to grow by 15% in 2023. Online gifting has become popular because it offers a convenient and cost-effective way for customers to shop for gifts and provides a greater selection of products and customization options.

Online gift-giving provides a hassle-free way for customers to shop for gifts without leaving the comfort of their homes. This is especially advantageous for those who face challenges such as limited mobility, a busy schedule, or living far away from family and friends.

With online shopping, customers can browse through a wider range of products and have them delivered directly to the recipient's doorstep, making the gift-giving experience more convenient and accessible for all.

Online retailers are also able to offer lower prices than traditional brick-and-mortar stores, due to lower overhead costs.

Online shopping allows customers to read reviews and ratings from other customers. Social proof about the products helps them make informed decisions about their purchases.

Consumer Trends and Behavior in Online Gift Giving

  • 1.

Personalization One of the main consumer trends in online gift-giving is the desire for personalization. Consumers want to give unique gifts tailored to the recipient's interests and preferences. This trend has led to the rise of personalized gift options , such as customizable jewelry, engraved items, and photo gifts.

  • 2.

Convenience Another trend in online gift-giving is the desire for convenience. Consumers want to purchase gifts quickly and easily, without spending much time searching for the perfect item. This trend has led to the popularity of Gift cards, Gift hampers, and Subscription boxes, which offer a curated selection of items based on the recipient's interests.

  • 3.

Need for Multiple Delivery Options Online retailers also offer several easy delivery options, including the ability to have gifts shipped directly to the recipient's address. This is especially convenient for people who live far away from their loved ones.

  • 4.

Sustainability & Ethical Practices Finally, consumers are increasingly concerned about sustainability and ethical practices. They want to purchase gifts from companies that prioritize sustainability, fair labor practices, and ethical sourcing. This trend has led to the rise of eco-friendly gift options, such as reusable bags, organic products, natural products, and sustainable clothing.

Taking Advantage of Online Gift-Giving Trends as a Marketer As a marketer, it's important to understand consumer trends and behaviors to create successful online gift-giving campaigns. Here are some strategies you can use to take advantage of these trends:

  • 1.

Personalization Offer personalized gift options, such as customized jewellery, monogrammed items, or personalized photo gifts. Use data analytics to create targeted marketing campaigns based on consumer preferences and interests.

Studies show that, 72% of consumers prefer to receive gifts that are personalized. 61% of consumers are more likely to shop with a retailer that offers personalized gift options.

58% of consumers are willing to pay more for a personalized gift.

  • 2.

Convenience Offer easy and convenient gift options, such as gift cards or gift boxes. Make sure your website is user-friendly and optimized for mobile devices.

  • 3.

Sustainability Highlight your company's commitment to sustainability and ethical practices. Use eco-friendly packaging materials and promote sustainable gift options on your website.

A Deloitte survey reported that 57% of consumers consider sustainability when making a purchase, and 54% are willing to pay a premium for sustainable products.

  • 4.

Social Media Use social media platforms to promote your online gift options and engage with your audience. Encourage customers to share their gift-giving experiences on social media and offer incentives for doing so.

As online gift-giving continues to gain popularity among consumers, marketers must stay abreast of the trends and behaviors driving this trend.
