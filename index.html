How Does Kodie Work?

Step 1

What is a Brand Page?

A brand page lets you run marketing campaigns, post ads, and launch promotions that showcase products and bring visitors to your store. With Kodie you can delight customers, build loyalty, re-engage buyers, convert gift recipients, and promote sales.

How does the brand page work?

After the recipient views the gift message, Kodie redirects them to a brand page featuring a product carousel, image or video ads. They can engage and click Shop Now to reach your store — while Kodie tracks every interaction for your analytics dashboard.
